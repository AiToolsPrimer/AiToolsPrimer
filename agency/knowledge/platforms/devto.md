## Rules (official)
- AI-assisted articles must disclose it (DEV's AI disclosure setting / the #ABotWroteThis tag) and must be checked for factual accuracy. Undisclosed low-effort AI content can get the account suspended.
- AI-assisted articles should not promote a business, program or course, including your own: keep the guide link as a reference, not a pitch.
- AI-generated comments are prohibited, including on your own posts: the community manager never auto-replies here.

## What works
- Practical, technical angles: how a tool behaves, what breaks, how to work around it. Code or screenshots. 4 tags max, lowercase.
- Canonical URL set to our guide.
