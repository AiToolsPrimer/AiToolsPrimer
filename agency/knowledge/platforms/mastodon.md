## How reach works
- No ranking algorithm: Home is chronological. Discovery happens through hashtags (followed hashtags, hashtag timelines), boosts (reposts), the Explore tab (trending posts/links/hashtags, moderated per server) and the "Local" and "Federated" timelines.
- Boosts are everything: a boost puts your post into someone else's followers' Home. Likes do nothing for reach.
- Hashtags are the search index: 2-4 relevant hashtags in CamelCase (#AITools, #PasswordSecurity) help both discovery and screen readers.

## Etiquette (enforced by the community, not by code)
- Alt text on every image. Content warnings (CW) for sensitive topics (scams with victims, violence, politics).
- No link-only posts; add a sentence of context. Cross-posted X content with broken "@handles" is frowned upon.
- Self-promotion is fine when it is useful and honest; say when something is ours.
- Declare bots/automation in the profile if the account is automated.

## What works for us
- Guides as short explainers with a link, 2-4 CamelCase hashtags at the end, an image with alt text.
- Reply to people who boost or ask questions; the community manager handles conversations here.
- Post in the European morning and the US morning.
