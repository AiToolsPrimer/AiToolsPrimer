## How it works
- The newsletter goes to subscribers; growth comes from Notes (Substack's feed) and recommendations between publications.
- Notes feed (changed late 2025): restacks are the main signal, then early likes; the feed no longer favours people you follow, it favours audience overlap. Frequent, short Notes grow faster.
- A Note with strong early engagement (reported: 16+ likes in 3 hours) keeps spreading.

## What works for us
- One weekly issue: the week's findings with the guide links. Notes: one finding per Note, no link in the Note body, link in a reply.
- Restack other newsletters in our niche with a comment.
