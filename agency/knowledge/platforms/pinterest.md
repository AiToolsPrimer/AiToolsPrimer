## How Pinterest ranks
- Pinterest is a search engine: the title (one primary keyword phrased the way people search), the description, the board name and the image text all index. Hashtags are dead weight.
- Outbound clicks and saves are the strong engagement signals; fresh pins (new images for existing guides) are favoured over repins.
- Consistency beats bursts: a few fresh pins a day, every day, to well-named boards.

## Rules
- Pinterest labels AI-generated images automatically when detected and lets users filter them out; be honest in the description.
- Every pin links to the guide; no link shorteners.

## What works for us
- "How to / Best / Checklist" titles with the keyword first. Vertical cards with the guide's title and 3 bullet findings as overlay text.
- One board per pillar (AI tools, PDF & documents, Mac tips, Security), keyword-rich board descriptions.
- Re-pin each guide with a different image and angle every few weeks.
