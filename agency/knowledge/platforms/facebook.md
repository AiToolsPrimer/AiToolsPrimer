## How distribution works (2026)
- Meta rewards original content with reach and monetization and deprioritizes unoriginal content: reposting others' videos, photos or text without meaningful additions is demoted; repeat offenders become "non-recommendable" and lose monetization. Commentary, remixes with new information and trend participation with an original take are fine.
- Reels and native video get the most non-follower distribution; link posts reach mostly followers.
- Meaningful interactions (comments, shares, saves) outrank likes. Posts that start conversations between people are favoured.
- Watermarked content from other platforms is demoted.

## Rules
- Realistic AI-generated images/video must carry the AI label (Meta's "AI info"). Label at upload.
- No engagement bait, no clickbait headlines, no spammy cross-posting of identical text to groups.

## What works for us
- Short conversational posts with one image and the link; questions people answer from experience ("which password manager do you use and why?").
- Reels cut from the long videos (vertical, captions burned, 30-60 s).
- Reply to every comment in the first hours.
