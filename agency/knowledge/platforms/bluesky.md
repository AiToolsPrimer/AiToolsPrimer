## How the feeds work
- No single algorithm. Following is chronological; Discover is algorithmic (your social graph, what your network engages with, early engagement velocity); anyone can build and subscribe to custom feeds. Bluesky's 2026 roadmap makes Discover the priority.
- Links are not penalized. Link cards (previews) are fine and get clicks.
- Replies and reposts count more than likes for Discover; "Show more like this" lets users tune the feed.
- Custom feeds are the growth lever: topical feeds (e.g. "AI tools", "privacy") pick up posts by keywords and hashtags, so a relevant hashtag or two is worth it here.

## Rules
- Alt text on every image (community expectation, and it feeds accessibility-based feeds).
- Labelers can flag spam; keep a human cadence (a few posts a day at most).
- AI-generated images: say so in the alt text or the post when they look photographic.

## What works for us
- Plain, specific posts with a link card to the guide. One idea per post.
- Starter packs and custom feeds: ask to be included in relevant packs; subscribe to feeds about our pillars and reply usefully there.
- Conversational replies to readers (the community manager handles these).

## Don'ts
- No hashtag walls (2 max). No cross-posted X-style threads with "1/" numbering: use the native thread (reply chain).
