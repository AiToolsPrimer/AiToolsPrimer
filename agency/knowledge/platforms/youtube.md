## How recommendations work (2026)
- Long videos: click-through rate (thumbnail + title) and then average view duration / retention decide reach; satisfaction surveys matter. Shorts: swipe-away rate, completion, shares.
- "Inauthentic content" policy (renamed July 2025, enforced hard since January 2026): mass-produced, templated, repetitive or re-uploaded content is demonetized and demoted. In October 2026 Shorts recommendations were changed to favour original content. AI use itself is not penalized; low-effort AI slop is.
- AI disclosure: a label is required for realistic altered/synthetic content; YouTube adds one itself when it detects significant photorealistic AI. The label does not change recommendations or monetization.
- Titles with the specific finding beat generic titles; chapters and a keyword-rich first line of the description help search.

## Rules
- Tick "altered or synthetic content" when the video shows realistic AI scenes or an AI presenter. Our presenter is declared as AI in the description.
- Shorts need "#Shorts" in the title or description to be classified reliably.
- No misleading thumbnails; no reused clips from other channels.

## What works for us
- 3-4 minute "we tested" videos with a verdict in the first 20 seconds, then the evidence, then "the full table is in the guide".
- Thumbnails: one face-free object + 3-5 words. Titles: the finding, under 60 characters.
- Shorts: the single most surprising result from each long video.
