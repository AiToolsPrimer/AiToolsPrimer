## How the feed ranks
- Dwell time is the top signal (how long people stay on the post), then comments (weighted far above likes), saves, and shares. The first 60-90 minutes decide most reach.
- Document (PDF carousel) posts get the longest dwell time and the highest engagement rate of any format.
- External links in the body cut reach sharply (reported ~60%); the standard move is the link in the first comment.
- Hashtags are weak; 1-3 relevant ones at most. Posting more than once a day cannibalizes reach.
- Comments the author answers within the first hour extend distribution.

## Rules
- Professional context: no clickbait, no outrage. Personal-profile posts outperform Page posts; a Page can reshare.
- AI-generated visuals of real-looking people should be disclosed.

## What works for us
- "What we found when we tested X" posts: first line is the finding, short lines, one lesson, a question at the end.
- Carousels: one idea per page, big type, page 1 is the hook, last page is "the full table is on the guide" (link in comment).
- Articles (LinkedIn Articles) for long-form repurposing, published through the browser operator.

## Don'ts
- No link in the body. No hashtag walls. No "agree?" bait. No posting during the US night.
