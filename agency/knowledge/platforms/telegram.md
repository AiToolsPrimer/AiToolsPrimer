## How reach works
- No feed algorithm: every subscriber receives every post. Growth comes from cross-promotion with similar channels, mentions elsewhere, and the channel link on the site and in bios.
- Too many posts cause mutes (more than ~14 a week); 3-7 a week is the comfortable range.
- Link previews render inline; the first line is the notification text, so it carries the hook.

## Rules
- Mark sponsored content and automation honestly in the channel description.
- No forwarding other channels' content wholesale.

## What works for us
- One guide per post: finding, two lines of why, link. An image when it adds information.
- Weekly digest post on Fridays.
