## Rules
- Answers must be helpful first; self-promotion is allowed when disclosed and relevant. Spam and link-dropping get answers collapsed and accounts banned.
- Quora has no explicit AI ban, but low-effort generated answers are collapsed; the agency produces a draft with the facts and you rewrite it in your words before posting.

## What works
- Answer the exact question, give the method and the finding, then "we published the full comparison here" as the last line.
