## How the feed ranks
- For You weighs replies and reply length heavily (replies over ~8 words are reported to count more than likes), then reposts/quotes, then likes. Images outperform text-only posts.
- Topic tags, not hashtags: one tag per post, a word or phrase without the # symbol, which routes the post into a topic feed. Posts with a topic tag are reported to get more views.
- Links: the earlier link penalty is reported reversed in 2026, but a bare link still underperforms a link wrapped in a real thought. Context first, link second.
- Early engagement velocity matters; posting when the US is awake helps.

## Rules
- Realistic AI-generated people or scenes must be labelled (Meta applies "AI info" labels; use the AI label on upload).
- No engagement bait. Reposted content from other platforms with watermarks is demoted.

## What works for us
- Opinionated but verified one-liners, then the reasoning in a reply. Ask a question.
- One topic tag that matches the pillar (e.g. "AI tools", "Privacy", "Mac tips").
- Carousels of 3-5 images for "x vs y" comparisons.
