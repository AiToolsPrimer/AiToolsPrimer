## How distribution works
- Medium's Boost program gives extra distribution to human-quality stories; fully AI-generated writing gets no enhanced distribution and reaches mostly your followers. AI-assisted stories must disclose it within the first two paragraphs. AI-generated stories cannot be paywalled (Partner Program).
- Follower count barely affects distribution; curation into topics does. Five relevant tags.
- Importing from the canonical URL keeps the canonical link to our guide (good for SEO); the agency prepares a new angle per guide, not a copy.

## Rules
- Disclose AI assistance in the first two paragraphs ("Written with AI assistance and checked against our tests").
- No affiliate links, no clickbait titles.

## What works
- Essays around a finding: the problem, what we tested, what surprised us, what to do. 700-1200 words, subheadings, one image.
- Publication submissions in our niche for reach.
