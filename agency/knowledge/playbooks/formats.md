label: Media formats and sizes
last_checked: 2026-10-20
---

| Use | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Landscape card (X, Bluesky, Mastodon, LinkedIn, Facebook, OG) | 1200x675 or 1920x1080 | text safe zone 10% inset |
| Square (Instagram feed, Threads) | 1080x1080 | |
| Portrait (Instagram, LinkedIn carousel pages, Threads) | 1080x1350 (4:5) | best feed real estate |
| Story / Reel / Short / TikTok | 1080x1920 (9:16) | keep text inside the central 1080x1420; UI covers top 250 px and bottom 400 px |
| Pinterest pin | 1000x1500 (2:3) | title text on image |
| YouTube thumbnail | 1280x720 | 3-5 words, high contrast |
| Channel banner / profile cover | 1500x500 (X), 1128x191 (LinkedIn page), 820x312 (Facebook), 2560x1440 (YouTube, safe 1546x423) | |
| Avatar | 1024x1024 | works everywhere |
| Podcast artwork | 3000x3000 | JPEG/PNG, under 512 KB |

Video: H.264 MP4, 24 or 30 fps, AAC 192 kbps, loudness around -14 LUFS, captions burned for vertical cuts, sidecar SRT for YouTube.
