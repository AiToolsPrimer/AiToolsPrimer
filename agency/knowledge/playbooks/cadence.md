Defaults the director starts from (UTC). The analyst rewrites these per platform from our own results after 30 days of data.

| Platform | Per week | Days | Hours UTC |
|---|---|---|---|
| X | 10-14 | daily | 13, 16, 20 |
| Bluesky | 10-14 | daily | 12, 15, 19 |
| Mastodon | 7-10 | daily | 8, 12, 17 |
| Threads | 7-10 | daily | 12, 15, 21 |
| LinkedIn | 3-4 | Tue-Thu + Mon | 7, 11, 16 |
| Facebook | 4-5 | Mon-Fri | 12, 15, 19 |
| Instagram | 4-5 posts + 3 Reels | Mon-Sat | 11, 14, 19 |
| TikTok | 4-5 | Mon-Fri | 12, 16, 20 |
| YouTube | 1-2 long + 4-5 Shorts | Tue/Thu long, Shorts daily | 14, 17 |
| Pinterest | 10-14 fresh pins | daily | 13, 20 |
| Telegram | 3-5 | Mon-Fri | 8, 17 |
| Articles (DEV, Hashnode, Medium, Substack) | 2-3 | Mon/Wed/Fri | 8 |

Quiet hours: nothing goes out 01:00-06:00 UTC. A long video takes a day of the render budget; the director never schedules two on the same day.
