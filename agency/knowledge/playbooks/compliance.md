## EU AI Act, Article 50 (applies from 2 August 2026)
- Providers of generative AI must mark outputs machine-readably (C2PA/watermarks). The agency keeps the generator's metadata on images and videos it produces.
- Deployers (us, when we publish) must clearly label deepfakes (realistic AI images/video/audio of people, places, events) and AI-generated text published to inform the public on matters of public interest when it has not had human review. Our texts always have human review (approval), and our visuals are illustrative, but we label realistic AI visuals and the AI presenter anyway.
- Visible labels: the EU icon / "AI generated" or "AI modified" wording, at the start of a video and at intervals; a line in the caption for images.
- Chatbots/assistants must say they are AI when it is not obvious: any AI persona account states it is an AI in the bio and when asked.
- Content created before 2 August 2026 needs no retroactive label.

## Platform labels (set at upload)
- Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads): "AI info" label for realistic AI media; AI-creator account label being tested.
- TikTok: AI-generated toggle for realistic AI visuals/audio; cannot be removed later.
- YouTube: "altered or synthetic content" disclosure for realistic AI; label added automatically if detected.
- Pinterest: automatic AI labels; be honest in descriptions.
- DEV: AI disclosure setting and #ABotWroteThis for AI-assisted articles; Medium: disclosure in the first two paragraphs.

## Honesty rules of the house
- Every number, price, limit or feature comes from the guide's verified facts or is not said. Say when something was checked.
- No affiliate links, no paid placements, no "sponsored by" ever (the site's rule).
- No fake reviews, no invented testimonials, no AI personas pretending to be human.
- Comments and replies: never on other people's content in bulk; one at a time, useful, with your approval.

## US FTC basics (for any future partnership)
- Material connections must be disclosed clearly and conspicuously; "#ad" at the start, not buried. We currently have none.
