## AI Tools Primer

Independent, plain-English guides to AI tools and everyday software: documents and PDF, images, video, business software, Windows, Mac and iPhone, and the things that happen to you online, from passwords and scams to malware and data leaks.

**Site:** [aitoolsprimer.com](https://aitoolsprimer.com)

### The rules the site runs on

- **Verify, never remember.** Every price, limit and feature is checked before it is published, and the page says when it was checked.
- **If I cannot verify it, I say so.** An honest gap beats a confident invention.
- **Nobody pays to be here.** No affiliate links and no paid placements anywhere on the site.

The site is built as an island city: two squares and twenty-five towers. Thirteen towers for the work you choose to do, twelve for the things that happen to you. Every tower is a subject, every floor a guide.
